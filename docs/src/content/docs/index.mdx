The GeekMagic SmallTV is a small cube with a 1.54" colour screen, an ESP inside, and a USB-C port. This firmware replaces the stock apps with three modes you switch between in a built-in web UI, and it updates over WiFi. The web UI header shows a chip badge for the board it's running on, and an optional scheduled night mode dims or turns off the backlight on an NTP clock and timezone you pick, without taking the device offline.

![The SmallTV running its three modes](/smalltv-mod/assets/screen.svg)

## The three modes

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Stock and crypto ticker" icon="seti:default">
    Price, change, percent change, and a sparkline for up to 8 rotating symbols. Data from Yahoo Finance with no backend, or your own webhook.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Claude usage meter" icon="seti:crystal">
    An animated mascot plus your 5-hour and 7-day usage bars with reset countdowns, fed over WiFi by the clawdmeter-daemon on your PC. The daemon can auto-discover several SmallTVs on the LAN and push usage to all of them at once.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Plane radar" icon="rocket">
    A scope centred on your location with nearby aircraft, headings, speed vectors, and callsigns, from the free adsb.lol or adsb.fi feeds or a LAN webhook.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Plus one thing that is not a mode: on every ESP32 board the firmware carries a [WireGuard client](/smalltv-mod/features/wireguard/), so you can reach the device from outside your network without opening a port to the internet. The ESP32-C2 and the 8 MB SmallTV Pro have it in their only image; on the NM-TV-154, whose image is the tightest fit of the three, it comes in a second image you pick at install time.

## More than the three modes

A [Home Assistant screen](/smalltv-mod/features/ha/) arrives over MQTT as a JSON draw list and the device shows it as a full 240×240 screen. A [notification](/smalltv-mod/features/notify/) takes the panel for a few seconds when a script pushes one over HTTP, then puts back whatever was showing. And a [theme clock](/smalltv-mod/features/themes/) replaces the screen with a clock face of your own: background art, animated sprites, clock and date text, and live values from JSON sources, installed as a `.stheme` package with no firmware rebuild.

![A Home Assistant screen pushed over MQTT, a notification overlay, and the Pixel Room theme clock](/smalltv-mod/assets/screen2.svg)

## Design a clock face in SmallTV Studio

[SmallTV Studio](https://giovi321.github.io/smalltv-studio/) is the visual editor for theme clocks. It runs in the browser with nothing to install. Stack layers on a 240×240 canvas, preview the clock at any simulated time, bind fields to JSON data, then export a `.stheme` package or send it to the device over WiFi.

[![SmallTV Studio editing the Pixel Room theme](/smalltv-mod/assets/studio.png)](https://giovi321.github.io/smalltv-studio/)
